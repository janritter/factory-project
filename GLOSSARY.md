# Course Platform

An online platform where learners buy courses from instructors.

## Language

**Purchase**:
A learner's one-time payment for a course, which gives them access to it.
_Avoid_: Subscription, order

**Refund**:
A request by a learner to get their money back for a Purchase. A person on our team reviews it by hand, and it ends as Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel, reversal, chargeback

**Refund window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which a learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period
