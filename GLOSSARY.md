# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Staff**:
The people at Cadence who decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Team (a team is a customer that buys seats), Admin (teams have admins too)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. Staff approve or decline it, and that decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff haven't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff have accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course at that moment.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Staff have turned down, with a Decline Reason. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request if the purchase still meets the usual rules for asking.

**Decline Reason**:
Staff's explanation, shown to the learner, of why their Refund Request was declined.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund Staff have approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
